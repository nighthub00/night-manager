# Third-party notices

## RoALT

Parts of NIGHT MANAGER are adapted from [RoALT](https://github.com/inspectsoftware/RoALT)
(Rust/TypeScript, ported to Python): the link parser (`features/launch_target.py`), reading
Roblox client logs (`features/roblox_logs.py`), the client tracker and recovery rules
(`features/client_tracker.py`, `features/auto_rejoin.py`), per-client limits
(`features/client_limits.py`), launch-time graphics settings (`features/launch_settings.py`),
free resizing (`features/free_resize.py`), Discord alerts and rich presence, the bandwidth
policy, Roblox update checks, performance profiles and the release-signing scheme.
